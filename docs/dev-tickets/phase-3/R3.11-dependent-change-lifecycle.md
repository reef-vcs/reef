# R3.11 Dependent change lifecycle

Phase 3 · Size L with decision 1, M without it · Depends on: R3.10 (stacks from jj's parent edges, `land::stack`, the gate's prefix, groups), R3.13 (the gate, `PROPOSAL_AMENDED`, `NOT_PROPOSED`), and builds on R3.09 (lifecycle rows, daemon transitions, `superseded_by`), R3.12 (instructions routed to the proposing session), R3.16 (summarized landings rewrite descendants), R3.25 (conflict objects and their `kind` seam), R3.27 (assignments keyed by change and kind, owners, the resolver queue), R3.28 (tiers on tree conflicts), R3.45 (the automatic status, the daemon's instructions), #259 (`Write::Rebase`, ADR 0071), #262 (`reef here`'s steps) and ADR 0039 (carries) · Fixes: #91 and #202 with decision 1 · Unblocks: R3.31 and R3.33 (the dependency facts they show)

## Context

The design's Stacks paragraph: "A dependent can be proposed on its own while its dependency is still proposed; it is a separate proposal linked to the dependency, with its own evidence and review, and the landing queue orders the two by the edge. Amending a change rebases its dependents onto the new snapshot and keeps their IDs, and a dependent that no longer applies cleanly carries a conflict object rather than blocking the amend. When a dependency is declined or withdrawn, each dependent returns to its owner with a conflict object naming the missing dependency, or to the resolver queue if the owner's session has ended. When a dependency is superseded, its dependents are rebased onto the superseding change."

Phase 3's exit criterion: "Amending the bottom change of a stack rebases the changes above it, preserves their IDs, and leaves a conflict object on any that no longer applies."

Earlier tickets left this one seams:

- **R3.09**: R3.11 "reads `superseded_by`, finds dependents' owners through the transition's `session_id`, and listens for `DECLINED`, `WITHDRAWN` and `SUPERSEDED`".
- **R3.10**: "R3.11 carries proposals of members left behind across the landing's rewrite, orders separately proposed dependents by these edges, and returns dependents of a declined or withdrawn member with conflict objects."
- **R3.16**: under `summary`, every landing rewrites descendants, whose proposals "read `amended` and [are] proposed again (R3.11 carries proposals across a dependency's rewrite)".
- **R3.25**: `ConflictKind` is `TREE` only; "R3.11 adds `CONFLICT_KIND_MISSING_DEPENDENCY`" with its own storage and fields.
- **R3.27**: assignments are keyed by change and kind; "R3.26 and R3.11 open assignments of their kinds through the same table and follower seam".
- **R3.45**: "R3.11 re-proposes a member a prefix landing rewrote, which lets a waiting stack land its prefix early."

The direction since 2026-11-09: agents are the users. A task split into a stack is often worked by several agents, one per change, each in its own workspace. Each must keep working while the changes below it move, and learn without polling when one of them is declined.

- **The literal criterion already holds for commits.** Every snapshot rebases descendants in its own transaction (`commit_snapshot`, `reef-lib/src/jj/snapshot.rs:632`), and so does every Reef write. After X's amend, Y had a new commit with the same change id, jj's conflict in its tree, a `tree` conflict object whose three terms named X's new commit (rebase destination), X's old commit (base) and Y's proposed commit, all `verified` (R3.25), and an R3.27 assignment `assigned` to Y's workspace session, reason `default`. Nothing in this ticket has to make the rebase, the id or the tree conflict happen.
- **Y's workspace did not follow (#202).** Y's directory kept X's old line with no conflict markers while `reef here` in it said `resolve`: the agent was told to resolve a conflict it could not see. Only `reef ws snapshot <Y>` brought the markers. A busy Y diverges instead (#91): snapshots hand out no carries (ADR 0039's consequences), so Y's next snapshot rewrites its old commit beside the rebased one. Both issues are open.
- **Proposals stop at every rewrite below them.** Y read `amended: true`; `reef land` refused `PROPOSAL_AMENDED` with `next: reef propose <Y>`; under `automatic = true` the service sets `needs`/`amended` (`landing/automatic/judge.rs:412`) and the daemon's instruction says "propose it again" (`landing/instruct.rs`). That holds for a clean rebase too, and R3.16's summarized landings make every landing one.
- **A declined dependency is silent, and the advice overrides the reviewer.** With X and Y proposed as a stack and X declined: `reef here` in Y said `land`; `reef land` refused `NOT_PROPOSED` naming X with the hint "proposing the head proposes the stack" and `next: reef propose <Y>` (`landing/mod.rs:349`); running it re-proposed the declined X (round 2) with no word about the decline (R3.10's stack propose takes members from `DECLINED`, `lifecycle/mod.rs:1032`).
- **Supersede moves nothing.** `TransitionChange` to `SUPERSEDED` records `superseded_by` (R3.09) and leaves X's dependents on X.
- **The pieces to build on exist.** `Write::Rebase` moves a set of commits with their workspaces and carries (`workspace/rewrite.rs`, ADR 0071); `carries` and `register_carries` (`rewrite.rs:700`, `:484`) compute a workspace's carry from the working copies before and after a transaction; `record_daemon_transitions` (`lifecycle/mod.rs:669`) records daemon transitions; `instruct::send` writes a daemon instruction with fixed templates and the one-unfinished-per-change limit; `conflict_assignments.kind` is a free `TEXT` column with one open row per (repository, change, kind) (`0017_conflict_assignments.sql`).

### What already holds, and what this ticket adds

Amending a change already rebases its dependents in the same operation, keeps their change ids, and leaves jj's conflict in any that no longer applies, which R3.25 reads as a `tree` conflict object and R3.27 assigns to the dependent's owner (Context, verified). This ticket keeps that mechanism and builds what an agent on a dependent needs around it: its files follow (decision 1), its proposal follows when its own work is unchanged (decision 2), it is told when it no longer applies (decision 5), and a dependency that leaves review returns it to its owner with a conflict object of its own (decisions 3 and 4). No new state is stored about stacks or edges: dependencies stay jj's parent edges (ADR 0078).

A **dependent** of change X, here, is an open proposal (`PROPOSED` or `QUEUED`) whose stack (R3.10: the change and its ancestors not on its destination) contains X. Drafts are not dependents: their owners are still working, and `reef here` reads their stack when they propose.

### Descendant workspaces follow an ancestor's snapshot (decision 1, fixes #91 and #202)

After a committed snapshot whose transaction moved other Reef workspaces' working-copy commits (jj's `rebase_descendants`), the snapshot path does what `Manager::rewrite`'s step 5 does for a write:

1. **Carries.** On the repository thread, in the same job that committed the snapshot, `carries(before, after, rows)` over the view's working copies before and after the transaction, and `register_carries` for every Reef workspace that moved within its change with another tree. The code moves from `workspace/rewrite.rs` to a shared `workspace/follow.rs` with both callers.
2. **Follow snapshots.** Each moved workspace gets a snapshot of kind `explicit`, as step 5's follow-up is (ADR 0039), queued after the ancestor's snapshot returns, descendants first. An idle workspace finds its working copy stale with no edits and checks the new tree out (ADR 0018's adopt arm): #202. A busy one carries its late edits onto the rebased commit in the same change (ADR 0039): #91.
3. **Not recursive.** A follow snapshot that itself rebases descendants (a carried merge in a middle member) registers carries and follows for those, once per workspace per operation; a workspace already queued is not queued twice.

The race ADR 0039 names (a descendant between its scan and its commit when the ancestor commits) stays the fourth arm, documented. Nothing changes in the API; `reef op log` shows the follow-up as an explicit snapshot.

Without this decision, an agent above a moving change reads, builds and tests its dependency's old files, is told to resolve a conflict whose markers are not on disk, and diverges its change if it was writing. The exit criterion's test (test 1) runs each member in its own workspace for this reason.

### Carrying a proposal across its dependency's rewrite (decision 2)

**The rule.** A `PROPOSED` change whose visible commit V differs from its recorded commit R is **carried** when:

1. V's parents are commits of the same changes as R's parents (the dependency was rewritten, not swapped), or the parents decision 6 rebased it onto;
2. rebasing R onto V's parents gives V's tree exactly (jj's merge of R's tree onto V's parents over R's parents' tree, computed in memory): the change's own diff is what was proposed;
3. V has no conflict.

Carrying records one transition, `PROPOSED → PROPOSED` at V, actor `DAEMON`, agent `reef-lifecycle`, the new cause **`DEPENDENCY`**, and the new `dependency` field: the moved parent's change id and its **event**, `AMENDED` (rewritten while not landed), `LANDED` (a landing rewrote it, R3.16), or `SUPERSEDED` (decision 6). The round is unchanged, as for any update. `amended` reads false again.

- **What does not carry.** Evidence and sign-offs bind to trees (R3.01, R3.07) and are not moved (R3.15 owns that); under `checks = "batch"` R3.45 asks for the new tree's evidence as for any proposal. A `QUEUED` change keeps its pinned commit and is dequeued `amended` as today; once `PROPOSED` it is carried like any other.
- **A dependent the rewrite left conflicted** is not carried: it stays `PROPOSED` and `amended`, with the `tree` conflict object and R3.27 assignment that already exist, and decision 5's instruction. After its owner resolves it, its tree is new work, so its owner proposes it again (R3.09).
- **A change's own rebase** (`reef rebase` on it, onto a new destination or a different parent change) is not a carry: ADR 0071's decision 6 stands (S-179: the agent states the new base). Its descendants are carried by rule 1.
- **When it happens.** A follower (`lifecycle/carry.rs`), woken by every operation as `lifecycle/observe.rs` is, settles 200 ms, then checks each repository's `PROPOSED` rows whose visible commit moved, at most 64 per pass (S-247). `LandChange` and the stack forms of `TransitionChange` carry the members they read first, under the lifecycle lock, so `reef land` right after an amend below never answers `PROPOSAL_AMENDED` for a clean dependent. One operation per pass per repository, kind `transition`, described `reef: carry 3 proposals (change <dep> amended)`.
- **Idempotent and undoable.** The rule is a function of R and V, so a pass after a crash does the same thing, and a carried change is never carried twice for one V. Undoing the dependency's amend moves V back, and the next pass carries again (the rule holds in both directions); undoing the carry's operation inverts its transitions as any transition undo does.
- **R3.45's prefix rule relaxes.** A stack whose upper member waits for requested evidence no longer holds back the passing prefix below it: landing the prefix rewrites the waiting member, and this rule carries it. Under `[history] message = "keep"` with a fast-forward nothing above moves at all.

### Missing dependencies (decision 3)

When a change in a dependent's stack is `DECLINED` or `WITHDRAWN`, the dependent **returns to its owner** as follows. Reef never moves the dependent off the dependency by itself: whether to drop the dependency's work, wait for it, or withdraw is the dependent owner's call.

- **The conflict object**, kind **`MISSING_DEPENDENCY`**, derived on every read like a `tree` conflict: one per (dependent, missing change), with `Conflict.dependency` = the missing change's id, its state, and the transition that took it out of review (who, when, reason). No terms or files; `arity` 0. Its id is `cfl_` + SHA-256 over `"dependency"`, the dependent's change id, the missing change's id and that transition's id, so a decline after a re-proposal is a new conflict. `ListConflicts` returns these beside tree conflicts: every dependent's with the default revset, else those of the dependents in the given revset; `GetConflict` gains `kind` (default `TREE`, the current behavior). It goes away by itself when the missing change is proposed again, the dependent is moved off it, or the dependent leaves review, lands or is abandoned.
- **The assignment**, R3.27's table with kind `dependency`, opened and closed by R3.27's follower from the derived objects (one open per dependent, naming the lowest missing change). Its owner and the queue are R3.27's rules unchanged: the newest live session that recorded the dependent; when that session has ended, a live session of the same client session, else after the 5-minute grace the **resolver queue**; `unowned` for a change Reef never recorded. Close reasons: `resolved` (the object went away while the dependent is still in review), `withdrawn` (the dependent left review; a new close reason), `abandoned`, `immutable`. R3.28's tiers and attempt caps apply to file conflicts and are not looked up for this kind (no paths, no sides); its claims are never `BLOCKED`.
- **The proposal.** The dependent stays `PROPOSED`. A `QUEUED` dependent is dequeued with the new landing reason **`DEPENDENCY`** naming the missing change. The gate refuses it with **`DEPENDENCY_LEFT_REVIEW`** (`FAILED_PRECONDITION`, metadata `change_id`, `state`, `transition_id`) in place of today's `NOT_PROPOSED` for that member, whose `next` re-proposed the declined change; the new `next` moves the lowest member above it off it: `reef rebase <member> --source --onto <the missing change's parent>`. The automatic status is `needs`, reason `dependency`.
- **The agent's next step.** `reef here` gains **`CHANGE_STEP_DEPENDENCY`**: "change <id> below it was declined (or withdrawn): move this change off it, or withdraw", with that `next`. It ranks with `resolve`, below `await_help` and `ask`.
- **Withdrawn is the same as declined**, at once. A withdrawn dependency that comes back closes the assignment as `resolved` on the follower's next look.

### Stack proposals and members that left review (decision 4)

`reef propose` of a stack (R3.10) refuses a member other than the revision itself that is `DECLINED` or `WITHDRAWN` **at its visible commit** (not revised since), with **`MEMBER_LEFT_REVIEW`** (`FAILED_PRECONDITION`, metadata `change_id`, `state`, `transition_id`), hint "it was declined at this commit by <actor>; revise it, propose it alone with `reef propose <id> --only` if you mean to put it back, or move this change off it", and decision 3's `next`. A member revised after its decline is proposed as R3.10 does: that is R3.09's "revise and propose again". `reef propose <member> --only` and `reef propose` on the declined change itself are unchanged.

### Telling the dependent's agent (decision 5)

The daemon writes one R3.12 instruction to the session that proposed the dependent (R3.12's routing) when:

- a change in its stack is declined or withdrawn (decision 3): "Change <dep> below change <id> was declined (or withdrawn), so <id> cannot land on it. Move <id> off it (reef rebase <member> --source --onto <parent>), or withdraw it (reef proposal withdraw <id>).";
- a rewrite of a change below it left it conflicted (decision 2): "Change <dep> below change <id> changed, and <id> no longer applies on it. Resolve the conflict (reef conflict show -r <id>), then propose it again (reef propose)."

On every destination, not only automatic ones. Author, template and limits are R3.45's (`instruct.rs`, S-228, S-229): actor `daemon`, agent `reef-lifecycle`, fixed text filled only with ids and a bookmark name cut to the safe alphabet, one per change, commit and text, none while one of the daemon's is unfinished on the change (which also keeps R3.45's `conflicted` instruction from repeating it). A clean carry writes nothing: there is nothing to do.

### Supersede rebases dependents (decision 6)

`TransitionChange` to `SUPERSEDED` on X, naming X2, also rebases X's dependents onto X2 in the same operation, as a `Manager::rewrite` write (`Write::Supersede`, with the transition, as `Write::Land` carries its transitions):

- **What moves**: every visible child of X's commit that is an open proposal or a descendant of one, with its descendants (`Write::Rebase`'s source mode, several roots), onto X2's visible commit. Their workspaces are snapshotted first and follow with carries (ADR 0031, 0039). A child that is an ancestor of X2 is not moved and is reported (`skipped`, reason `ancestor_of_successor`).
- **Proposals**: a moved dependent that applies cleanly is carried in the same operation (decision 2, event `SUPERSEDED`); one that conflicts keeps its proposal `amended`, gets its `tree` conflict and assignment (R3.27), and decision 5's instruction.
- **Who**: whoever may supersede (R3.09: on the socket, anyone; remotely, the token that proposed X). The dependents' owners are not asked: the design says they are rebased. S-244 records it.
- **Undo** of the operation reverts the transition, the rebase and the carries together.
- **A superseded change with no visible commit** has no children to move; its dependents were already moved by jj when it was abandoned. Nothing is done and the response says so.
- `TransitionChangeResponse` gains `moved` (each dependent's change, new commit, carried or conflicted).

### Linked proposals and landing order (no new behavior)

R3.10 already gives what the row's first sentence asks: a dependent proposed with `--only` while its dependency is proposed is its own proposal, with its own evidence and review; the link is the parent edge; `reef land` on the dependent lands its stack, the dependency first, and batches keep a stack's queued members in one group ordered by the edges. This ticket adds the link to the API (`ProposalEntry.dependencies`, below) and a test (test 6), not a mechanism.

### Threat model rows

- **S-243 A dependency's author moves a dependent's proposal.** X's agent amends X; Y's proposal follows without Y's agent. Fixed for what lands: only when Y's own diff is exactly what was proposed; evidence and sign-offs bind to trees and do not follow; the transition names X and the event; Y's agent may withdraw.
- **S-244 Supersede rebases other agents' work.** Recorded in one undoable operation with the superseding caller; workspaces follow with carries, never losing edits; remotely only X's proposing token may supersede (R3.09).
- **S-245 Overriding a decline through a dependent.** A dependent's stack proposal re-proposed a declined dependency. Fixed: `MEMBER_LEFT_REVIEW`; `next` no longer says to propose it.
- **S-246 Text in a dependent's agent's context.** The instructions are fixed templates over ids, as S-228; the decline's reason is never copied into them (`reef conflict show` shows it, marked untrusted).
- **S-247 Work by size.** At most 64 carry checks per pass and one operation per pass per repository; a follow snapshot per moved workspace per operation; missing-dependency reads are an index join over open proposals and the few declined or withdrawn changes.
- **S-248 A follow snapshot writes a descendant's files.** ADR 0039's re-stat applies; a file changed after the scan fails the check-out with nothing written and the carry is kept.
- **S-249 Churn.** A withdraw and re-propose loop opens and closes dependents' assignments and could flood instructions; the per-change limit and R3.27's reopen window bound it.

## Tests

In `crates/reef-lib/` (`tests/jj_writes.rs`): 

1. `carry_check`: a clean rebase onto an amended parent is `Carried`; an edit by the dependent after it is `Not(own_edit)`; a rebase onto a different parent change is `Not(parents)` unless allowed; a conflicted rebase is `Not(conflicted)`.

In `crates/reef-daemon/` (`tests/dependents.rs`, a policy with `[landing] checks = "proposal"` unless noted):

2. **`amend_bottom_of_stack`** (the exit criterion): A, B, C stacked, each in its own workspace, proposed as a stack; A amended in its workspace. B and C keep their change ids and are not divergent; B, which still applies, is carried (one `dependency` transition, `amended: false`) and its directory holds A's new content; C, which no longer applies, has a `tree` conflict object whose terms name A's new commit, an assignment to C's session, conflict markers on disk, `reef here` `resolve`, and one daemon instruction.
3. `busy_descendant_carries` (#91): B writes a file not yet snapshotted while A snapshots: one commit for B, not divergent, with A's change and B's late file.
4. `landing_carries_dependents`: under `[history] message = "summary"`, A lands alone; B (proposed `--only`) is carried with event `landed` and lands next with no `reef propose`.
5. `declined_dependency`: A declined; B and C get `missing_dependency` conflicts, one `dependency` assignment each to their sessions, the instruction, `reef here` `dependency` with its `next`; `reef land C` refuses `DEPENDENCY_LEFT_REVIEW`; `reef propose C` refuses `MEMBER_LEFT_REVIEW`; running the `next` moves B and C off A and closes the assignments `resolved`; a queued dependent is dequeued `dependency`.
6. `linked_dependent_lands_in_order`: B proposed `--only` on a proposed A by another agent; `reef land B` lands A then B in one operation; `ProposalEntry.dependencies` of B names A.
7. `withdrawn_then_back`: A withdrawn then proposed again: the assignment closes `resolved`; one instruction only.
8. `ended_owner_queues`: B's workspace released, then A declined: B's `dependency` assignment is queued after the grace (paused clock).
9. `supersede_moves_dependents`: A superseded by A2: B moved onto A2 and carried (event `superseded`), its workspace following; C conflicting on A2 keeps `amended` with a tree assignment; one undo reverts all of it.
10. `automatic_prefix_lands_early` (`checks = "batch"`, `automatic = true`, a scripted executor holding C's check): A and B land while C waits; C is carried and lands when its check passes.

In `crates/reef-vcs/` (`tests/cli.rs`, `tests/schema.rs`): `reef proposal show`'s dependencies and carry line, `reef conflict show --kind dependency`, `reef here`'s step, `reef proposal supersede`'s moved list; text, `-o json` and piped, validated against `reef schema`.

In `crates/reef-api/`: the digest at revision 41; `just api` and `just api-breaking`. In `web/`: the words test.

## Out of scope

- **Writing a conflicted rebase onto the destination at landing** (R3.13's dequeue `conflicted`): the agent's own `reef rebase` writes it and R3.27 assigns it (ADR 0071, decision 5), and R3.45's instruction says so. Not a dependent's lifecycle.
- **Carrying evidence or sign-offs** to a carried tree: R3.15.
- **Moving a dependent off a missing dependency automatically**: the owner's call (decision 3).
- **Dependents of an undone operation** (`UNDONE_OPERATION`): R3.26.
- **A `stack` policy input**: R3.24.
- **The proposal view and inbox**: R3.31 and R3.33 read the fields above.

## Risks

- **Decision 1 touches the snapshot path** every workspace uses. Mitigated by reusing step 5's code as is, landing it first with its own tests, and the Linux run.
- **A carry the agent did not expect.** Mitigated by the exact-diff rule, the transition's named cause, and evidence and sign-offs not following.
- **Instructions an agent cannot act on** when a dependency comes back quickly. Mitigated by one per change and the assignment closing itself; the instruction's `next` is still valid (moving off is never wrong, only sometimes unneeded).
- **Numbers** collide with R3.31's at merge; the second to merge renumbers.

## Choices this brief made, and why

- **No new mechanism for rebasing or ids**: jj already rebases dependents and keeps ids in the same operation; the work is what the agents above need.
- **Carry only a dependent's unchanged diff on the same parent changes**, because that is the case where the dependent's agent has nothing to say, while an agent that moves its own change states the new base (ADR 0071).
- **Missing dependencies derived, assignments stored**, as R3.25 and R3.27 split it: the stack and the lifecycle say whether a dependency is missing; only who should act is Reef's own fact.
- **Return without moving**: dropping a dependency's work changes what the dependent is, which only its owner can judge.

## Departures in the implementation

Found while building it (2026-11-19):

1. **No `workspace/follow.rs`.** Decision 1 reuses `rewrite.rs`'s `carries` from the snapshot path (`Manager::carry_moved`, `follow_moved` in `workspace/snapshot.rs`), skipping workspaces a write has paused and workspaces on the snapshot's own change, which ADR 0018 still diverges.
2. **Supersede is `Write::Rebase` with a new `RebaseMode::Children`** and the transition carried in its `OpMeta`, rather than a `Write::Supersede`; the carries are recorded right after it, outside the lifecycle lock, so undoing the supersede leaves them (ADR 0084's consequences).
3. **`MissingDependency` lives in `lifecycle.proto`**, shared by `Conflict.dependency` and the new `Workspace.missing_dependency`, with `move_change_id` and `move_onto` in place of a parent id: the step needs the command without a second read. A new assignment close reason, `CONFLICT_ASSIGNMENT_REASON_WITHDRAWN`, says the dependent left review itself.
4. **`ListConflicts` lists `missing_dependency` conflicts on the first page of a head read with the default revset** only (at most 200), and `kinds` filters; `GetConflict` with `kind` reads one by revision.
5. **The carry check runs before a landing, before a stack proposal and at the start of each automatic pass** (review findings 6 and 1), so no clean dependent is refused, told to propose again, or misread.
6. **R3.45's prefix rule is unchanged.** Landing the prefix below a change that waits for evidence makes the gate record that change `blocked`, which the automatic service then reads as returned; relaxing it needs the gate's write to leave a waiting member alone, a follow-up. Test 10 is not built.
8. **From the review** (findings in the PR): a declined or withdrawn change is carried too (`declined → declined`), so a rebase below it never counts as its owner's revision, and the missing-dependency read judges it by its own diff; `move_off` walks past every member above that left review, and the conflict id names the change to move (merges re-notify); lookups of a change's assignment by change alone read its `tree` row; instructions go out from the carry follower, outside the assignment lock; a supersede whose rebase is refused is recorded alone (`rebase_refused`) and `moved` always lists the dependents it left; a follow snapshot is queued at most once per workspace and its carry starts from the view's commit; a failed read closes no `dependency` assignment, which reopens within R3.27's window; `reef conflict show --kind` takes `missing_dependency` too.
7. **Tests.** `reef-lib`'s `carry_check_follows_only_a_dependencys_rewrite`; `reef-daemon`'s `files::idle_descendant_follows_an_ancestors_snapshot` and `busy_descendant_carries_through_an_ancestors_snapshot` (#202, #91), and `dependents.rs`: `amend_bottom_of_stack` (the exit criterion), `declined_dependency_returns_the_dependents`, `landing_carries_the_dependent`, `supersede_moves_the_dependents`, and from the review `a_rebased_decline_still_holds` and `moving_off_skips_every_declined_change`. R3.10's `withdrawn_middle_returns_the_rest` now expects reason `dependency`. Tests 7 and 8 (a withdrawn dependency coming back, an ended owner's queue) are covered by R3.27's own owner tests and the derived conflict closing itself; they are not repeated.

## Decisions for the user

**Answered 2026-11-19**: the user accepted 1 to 6 as recommended.

1. **Fold #91 and #202 into this ticket** (descendant workspaces follow an ancestor's snapshot: idle ones check the new tree out, busy ones carry their edits; size L). *Recommended*: fold in; without it the exit criterion holds only for commits, and an agent above an amended change reads stale files, is told to resolve markers it cannot see, or diverges its change. Alternative: leave them as their own issues, size M, with the exit-criterion test run on dependents no workspace holds.
2. **Carry a dependent's proposal across its dependency's rewrite**: the daemon records the new commit when the dependent's own diff is unchanged, it sits on the same parent changes (or decision 6 moved it), and it has no conflict, with cause `dependency` naming the dependency; evidence and sign-offs do not follow; an agent's own `reef rebase` still needs a new `reef propose` (ADR 0071). *Recommended*: as stated. Alternatives: carry any clean rebase, including the change's own `reef rebase` (reverses ADR 0071's decision 6); or carry nothing and let the agent re-propose, as today, told by R3.45's instruction only on automatic destinations.
3. **What "returns to its owner" means for a dependent of a declined or withdrawn change**: it stays proposed and is held from landing, with a derived `missing_dependency` conflict and an R3.27 assignment of kind `dependency` (its owner, else the resolver queue after the grace), tierless; Reef never moves it off the dependency. *Recommended*: as stated. Alternatives: the daemon withdraws the dependent (it leaves review and the inbox); or the daemon rebases it off the dependency at once, leaving a tree conflict when it no longer applies.
4. **A stack proposal refuses a member declined or withdrawn at its current commit** (`MEMBER_LEFT_REVIEW`); proposing that member alone, or after revising it, still works. *Recommended*: as stated; today a dependent's `reef propose` silently re-proposes a change a reviewer declined. Alternative: keep R3.10's behavior and only change `reef land`'s advice.
5. **Tell the dependent's agent with a daemon instruction (R3.12, R3.45's templates and limits) on every destination**, when its dependency is declined or withdrawn, or a dependency's rewrite left it conflicted; nothing for a clean carry. *Recommended*: as stated; a proposing agent waits on its instructions (`wait_for_operations` kind comment), not on assignment operations. Alternative: assignments and `reef here` only, as for other tree conflicts, with instructions only on automatic destinations as R3.45 has them.
6. **Supersede rebases the dependents onto the successor in the same undoable operation**, moving their workspaces with carries, whoever supersedes. *Recommended*: as stated (the design's sentence). Alternative: supersede records only, and each dependent's owner gets decision 5's instruction with `reef rebase <dep> --source --onto <successor>` as its `next`.
